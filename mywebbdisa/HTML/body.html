KTM is a well-known motorcycle manufacturer based in Austria, famous for producing high-performance bikes designed for both off-road and street use. The company, officially called KTM AG, started in 1934 and has grown into one of the leading brands in the global motorcycle industry. KTM is especially recognized for its dominance in off-road racing, including motocross, enduro, and rally competitions. Their bikes are built to be lightweight, powerful, and durable, which makes them popular among both professional racers and everyday riders. One of their biggest achievements is their long-standing success in the Dakar Rally, where KTM has won many titles. In addition to dirt bikes, KTM also produces street motorcycles under series like Duke and RC. These bikes combine aggressive design with strong performance, appealing to riders who want speed and sharp handling. KTM’s slogan, “Ready to Race,” reflects their focus on performance and competition. Today, KTM continues to innovate with new technology, including electric bikes and advanced engineering, keeping the brand at the forefront of the motorcycle world.

The brand signature colors are orange and black wich represent power, energy, and boldness.
